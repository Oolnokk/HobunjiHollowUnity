About this tool
Calls CavernSculptor.carveMazeCavern(opts, rng) — the real engine at docs/js/cavern-sculptor.js — directly, and renders the result with the real InteriorSceneBuilder.buildCarvedCavernMesh and the real in-game dark-cavern lighting values (see game.js's loadBuildingScene, wallStyle === 'cavern' branch). This is the actual den render, not an approximation — tweak sliders, hit Regenerate, and judge with your own eyes. "Copy opts as code" gives you a JS object literal to hand off for editing cavern-sculptor.js's DEFAULT_OPTS.
