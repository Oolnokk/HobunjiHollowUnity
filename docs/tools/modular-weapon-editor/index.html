Directed head and haft mounts

Heads and hafts require matching tang or eye lines to assemble.

Drag from one end of a mounting line to the arrow tip directly on the sprite. Arrows align direction-to-direction, then automatically rotate the head and stretch it only along the head arrow axis to match the haft line; its perpendicular width stays unchanged. Gold lines are tangs; aqua lines are eyes/sleeves. All lines use the original 450 × 1204 sprite coordinates.

In Draw mode, drag base to arrow tip. In Move mode, drag the crosshair to shift the whole line. In Rotate mode, drag the circle on its short arm to turn the line around its midpoint without changing its length.

Line length is the distance from its fixed origin (base) to its arrow tip. Use the number box, its native arrows, or the − / + buttons to adjust length without changing direction.

A haft can have tang lines, eye lines, or both; only the matching type accepts each head. Explicit exclusions take precedence even for compatible lines.

Selected head + haft corrections

Layering applies only to this head–haft combination, including all matching mounts. Authored initial choices put eye/sleeve heads above hafts and tang-mounted heads below them; either choice can be edited and saved independently.

There are no longer global transformations. The matching arrows supply initial translation, rotation, and scaling only along the line direction. These controls adjust only this exact combination, on top of its automatic transform.

Make a modular head or shaft from a full weapon


Load a complete PNG, draw around any pixels with a finger, stylus, or mouse, then remove the selected region or keep only the selection. Repeat on the remaining image; Undo and Reset protect the original artwork. Export retains the game’s 450 × 1204 coordinates and all surviving RGBA colors/transparency.

















Paint a fat black stroke from one metal piece to another, then Hollow & fill: metal under the stroke is kept, the rest keeps a black rim wherever it meets empty space, separate unshaded pools touching it get joined by an unshaded channel, and every metal pixel touching it races to grow its shaded or unshaded tone inward, carrying the parts’ shading bands into the bridge. The bridge’s own edges facing the light get an unshaded highlight band. Undo works here too.
