Placement Rulesfor the generator

The generator looks for a flat, fully open rectangle (footprint + clearance on every side) by random search — keep the footprint small and clearance low (1–2) for a locale to reliably find room in busy wilderness terrain. A locale that fails to place in one zone is retried in its other allowed zones.
