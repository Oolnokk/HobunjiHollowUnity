Leg bend (invisible hip/thigh/calf authoring)
Load an NPC to author a leg bend.
Each foot hangs off an invisible 2-bone leg running from the avatar's posterior anchor down to the foot (see docs/js/leg-bones.js) — the cyan/orange lines trace hip -> knee -> foot for the left/right leg. Unauthored, the knee just marks the straight-line midpoint and the leg tracks the foot automatically through the gait. "Edit leg bend" attaches a rotate-only gizmo to the left thigh; the right leg mirrors it sideways automatically. The edit applies to the currently loaded NPC's species+gender and is written into this session's live config — use the copy/download buttons to get the exact proceduralFeet.legBend block to paste into scratchbones-config.js.

Import a GLB to inspect and replace its materials.
Open this directly with the always-visible Foot Models button beside Placement. GLBs are kept for this browser session and replace the procedural foot on their assigned side. Imported models preserve their proportions, are normalized to the procedural foot height and then enlarged to 200%, centered in X/Z, and seated on the same jump-safe local contact baseline. Each detected GLB material now uses the furniture author’s surface-PNG workflow: choose a repository/custom PNG, optionally adaptive-shade-fill it with body color, Kenkari black, Tletingan bone white, or a custom color, then choose original/generated UVs, stretch, or tile mapping, quarter-turn rotation, and PNG transparency. GLBs without UVs automatically receive box-projected UVs, and PNG overrides normalize glossy or metallic source materials to a matte foot surface. A “Both feet” import is cloned without mirroring; use separate left/right files when the models are asymmetric.
Normal + sprint transformsBlending
neutral 1.00 · forward 0.00 · backward 0.00 · left 0.00 · right 0.00
The four normal transforms are 100% movement endpoints relative to the character’s own facing. Sprint Forward is one separate authored sprint endpoint. At runtime that one sprint pose is spatially rotated around the character’s local vertical axis to follow backward, sideways, and diagonal travel, so you do not need four sprint keyframes. Select any endpoint to preview it without rotating the locomotion root, then edit it with the 3D gizmo or exact fields. Normal runtime weights come from actual velocity projected into character-local axes—not the camera or world. In Drunken movement, stopping blends toward the opposite normal endpoint by Recoil distance at Recoil speed, then returns at Rebalance speed. Idle motion slowly shifts toward low-intensity random combinations of the four normal transforms.

Imported from docs/game.js. Regular uses the game’s 238 px/s target speed, 980 acceleration, 1320 sharp-turn acceleration, 1850 stopping force, 0.18 cardinal bias, and joystick response (56 px radius, 0.14 deadzone, 0.82 response curve), scaled to this 3D preview. Drunken motion modifies that same pipeline rather than replacing its controls. Its four default endpoint transforms are loaded from the supplied movement settings; stop recoil uses their character-relative opposites, and idle rocking blends slowly toward seeded random character-relative directions. Sprinting uses one editable Sprint Forward transform, rotated around the character’s local vertical axis for every travel direction.
