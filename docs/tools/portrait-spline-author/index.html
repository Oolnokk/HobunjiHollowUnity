Rigid portrait pixels vs. flexible spline pixels

The mask starts with every opaque hair/beard pixel that overlaps the selected head silhouette assigned to the rigid group. Brush adds rigid pixels; eraser returns pixels to the moving spline. Rigid pixels never bend or move off the portrait.

Rigid · anchored to portraitFlexible · follows spline

Yellow knots position the resting spline in 2D; cyan traces its directional 3D pose. Edit the mask on the artwork in the front or rear view. The two pixel groups use the exact same sprite and only the flexible group gets 3D deformation.

The rigid region stays fixed; the flexible hair gradually gains motion across the attachment blend distance. The tip controls the middle knot, never the reverse, while the head root remains anchored.

3D directional blend-space author
