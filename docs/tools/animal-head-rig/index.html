Animal variables

Unknown/new animal fields are preserved. This remains usable as the general animal-variable editor while authoring the rig.

Rig paint

Brush/Fill only reduce the selected channel below Influence. “Toward Influence” restores it toward the current Influence weight. Painting Influence resets both channels in the touched cells so they track the new Influence again. Stretchability also limits yaw head turns in either direction.
Bucket flood-fills connected opaque sprite cells close to the selected paint-source art. Brush strength applies to fill as well.
Expand is for the head Influence workflow. Shoulder Influence already defaults to full strength on the separator’s right side.

Shoulder-pet presentation

BEFORE / Bind is fitted to the animal exactly as it exists in the PNG. AFTER / Pose is where those same seven bind points move on the shoulder. Only the active line’s nodes are draggable; both remain visible. The separator’s X and Z rotation define pixel ownership; rotating it does not rotate or move either spline. Right/Composite source switches the three paint channels to the shoulder rig: Influence defaults to 100% on the separator’s right side, while Compressibility and Stretchability reduce local spline compression/stretch independently. The full rectangular strip participates, including transparent pixels.
