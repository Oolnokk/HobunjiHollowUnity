3. Floor plan tools

Paint floor footprint. Collider = invisible blocker (red). Door tile = an auto-rotated Wooden Door on a floor tile (it bridges the two unpainted tiles beside it, faces the lone painted neighbour at a dead end, faces north with none; NPCs open it by walking up, the player opens it by hand). Exit/vendor zones below. Paint exit markers on the unpainted tile immediately OUTSIDE the floor footprint. The exit retains its authored tile even without a visible floor slab, and the physical door sits on the boundary next to the painted floor. Erase floor under a single-neighbor exit to trim an unnecessary protrusion without moving the exit. Legacy exits painted on floor tiles remain supported.

3a. Ceiling & gables

Rooms get a flat ceiling at wall height (hidden in this editor unless Preview ceiling is on, where it shows see-through). With the Gable tool, click next to a wall to raise that whole wall stretch into a triangular gable; click it again to remove it. Opposite gables make a pitched roof; a lone gable slopes down to the flat wall facing it. Rise edits apply to gables you add next.

3b. Exit zones

To place a doorway on the wall edge, use Paint exit (add) or Move exit (replace), then click a blank grid cell immediately outside the floor. The cell is saved as the exit position even without visible floor. Direction is inferred from the neighboring floor; choose it yourself at corners. Spawn col/row set where you arrive on the destination map.
New interiors include an empty one-tile border for outside exits. Existing rooms need at least one blank grid cell beside their floor edge; enlarge the grid or erase an exit-only floor spur if necessary.
