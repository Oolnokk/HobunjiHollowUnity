2 · Root growth settings

Arc concavity: 0 = straight vertical rise; 1 = branches hug the root-covered floor longer before sweeping upward toward the furniture. Seeded variation keeps the curves organic.

Growth starts at actual floor-adjacent furniture parts, then travels through their connections: table legs before tabletop, arch posts before the overhang. Higher pieces retract first. Furniture roots follow a connected path through each original piece. Thin details narrow the roots; broad pieces receive winding knots; every touching neighbour gets a route. Detail preservation controls sampling and finer surface strands. Knot density controls interior winding. The minimum radius is a preference: roots can thin further to fit very narrow details. Floor density still layers the original floor Wrap. For furniture, roots are ray-shrinkwrapped inside its hidden physical surfaces: inset controls the near-surface clearance; volume fill depth spreads successive layers farther into the object's interior (clamped at thin walls). Authored furniture particles begin emitting once growth reaches 100% and switch off when it retracts.
