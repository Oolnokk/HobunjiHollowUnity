With floor protection enabled, the detector is allowed to touch the floor and every chaotic pick hit is clipped at a gently varying floor band. This evens the floor without smoothing the walls. Branching Maze Splines
The maze begins with one through-route. Each additional branch attaches to an existing spline, bends through XZ, and can reconnect to another route to form loops. The same wall-detection sphere excavates every branch.
Floor Grid Wall Snap
Wall snap claims the playable floor tiles first. The cull pass removes untouched slab tiles to black void. With Stitch culled edges enabled, triangles are clipped exactly at the cull boundary instead of being removed by centroid, then the exposed cut is sealed with new faces that connect the surviving edge from tip to tip. Restore Culled Mesh remains reversible.
